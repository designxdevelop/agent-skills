# DXD Skills privacy policy

Effective date: September 30, 2026.

This policy covers the DXD Skills plugin published by Design X Develop LLC.
DXD Skills is a collection of instructions and supporting files for coding
agents. It does not include a DXD-operated server, user account system, analytics
service, or telemetry endpoint.

## Information used and its purpose

When you invoke a skill, your agent may process the information needed for your
request: prompts, source code, project files, documents, session history,
browser content, and tool results. These materials may contain personal
information, such as names, email addresses, or information you include in a
document. Skills use this information to carry out the requested engineering,
design, research, or writing task.

Some workflows create local reports, session exports, screenshots, or network
captures. Network captures can contain authentication or personal information;
the relevant skill requires redaction before analysis or sharing. Do not include
secrets or unnecessary personal information in material you ask an agent to
process.

Design X Develop does not receive your prompts, project files, or tool results
through a collection service built into this plugin. The plugin does not add
tracking, advertising, or a sale of personal information.

## Agent hosts and other recipients

Your chosen agent host and model provider process inputs and tool results under
their own privacy policies and your account settings. Using a hosted model can
send local content to that provider; installing this plugin does not make a
hosted agent run entirely on your device.

Depending on your request and the tools you enable, a skill may interact with
services such as GitHub, Paper, or a website's API. Those services receive the
information needed for that interaction. For example, publishing code sends it
to the selected Git remote, and a design task may use a connected design service.
The host's permissions and approval controls continue to apply. These providers
govern their own processing and retention.

## Storage and retention

DXD Skills has no separate database or DXD-operated storage for your task data,
so Design X Develop does not retain a copy through the plugin. Files and reports
created in your workspace remain until you remove them. Temporary exports may
remain until the workflow or your system deletes them; the plugin does not
provide automatic deletion for every artifact.

Conversation records, model inputs, and data sent to connected services are
retained according to those providers' policies and your settings. Design X
Develop does not control or promise a retention period for those records.

## Your controls

You can choose which skills to invoke, which workspace and files the agent can
access, and which tools or service connections to enable. Review proposed
changes and external actions through your host's approval controls. You can
remove local reports and exports, disconnect tools, or uninstall the plugin.
Use the relevant provider's controls to manage or delete data held by your
agent host, model provider, or connected service.

## Contact and changes

For questions about this plugin or this policy, open an issue at
[the DXD Skills repository](https://github.com/designxdevelop/agent-skills/issues).
GitHub issues are public; do not include private data. For private questions,
email [hello@designxdevelop.com](mailto:hello@designxdevelop.com). Information you
send in a support request is used to respond to that request and is handled by
the support channel you choose; do not send passwords or authentication tokens.

Policy updates will be published on this page with a revised effective date.
